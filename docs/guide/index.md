# Guide

The guide covers one concept per page, in the order a build meets them.

<div class="grid cards origamy-links" markdown>

-   📐 **[Coordinates](coordinates.md)**

    The paper's frame, exact numbers in a tower of square roots, and the constructions that find fold lines.

-   🐍 **[Fold scripts](scripts.md)**

    Python that folds a `Paper`, runs in its own process, and records the script line behind each step.

-   ✋ **[Actions](actions.md)**

    Valley and mountain folds, unfolds, precreases and turning over, with flap points, layers and queries.

-   🪁 **[Collapses](collapses.md)**

    Many creases folded at once, and the named moves: reverse, petal and squash folds.

-   🕊️ **[Shaping](shaping.md)**

    Shape steps and tilts that turn folded paper out of the plane, as hands spread a crane's wings.

-   🔎 **[Feedback](feedback.md)**

    Refusals and warnings: the facts a build states, its rules, and the layer solver.

-   🖼️ **[Pictures](pictures.md)**

    The pictures every build draws, the x-ray, and the 3D viewer.

-   📦 **[Exports](exports.md)**

    The FOLD file every build writes, with exact coordinates as data.

</div>
